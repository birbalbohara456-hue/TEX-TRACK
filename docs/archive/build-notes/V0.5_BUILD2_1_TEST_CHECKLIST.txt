TexTrack ERP v0.5 Build 2.1 — Focused Test Checklist

A. JWO component rate
1. Open JWO and enter Component Allocation.
2. Select a component previously used with a positive JWO rate. Confirm Rate defaults to the latest stored rate.
3. Enter Required Quantity and press Enter. Confirm focus moves to Rate.
4. Edit Rate and press Enter. Confirm Amount = Quantity x Rate and next blank component row opens.
5. Save JWO, reopen it, and confirm Rate and Amount remain stored.
6. Confirm blank Component Stock Item + Enter still closes Component Allocation.

B. Material Out rate
1. Select the newly saved JWO.
2. Confirm Material Out Rate prefills from the linked JWO component rate.
3. Edit Rate and confirm Amount recalculates.
4. Save, reopen, and confirm Material Out retains its own Rate snapshot without changing JWO.

C. Cancellation migration
1. Restart TexTrack and confirm migration 011 applies.
2. Open an unlinked active Material Out and press Alt+X.
3. Enter a short reason and cancel.
4. Confirm no PostgreSQL varchar(30) error appears.
5. Confirm voucher remains in list as Cancelled and JWO pending quantity reopens.

D. Layout regression
1. Confirm Material Out uses compact JWO-like field proportions.
2. Confirm no lookup extends outside the voucher.
3. Re-test Enter, Esc, Alt+S, Alt+X and Alt+D.
