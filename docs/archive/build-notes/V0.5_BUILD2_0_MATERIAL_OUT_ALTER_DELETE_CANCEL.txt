TexTrack ERP v0.5 Build 2.0 — Material Out Alter, Delete and Cancel

Base
- Built from v0.5 Build 1.9 supplied by the user.

Material Out list
- Enter or double-click opens a saved voucher.
- Alt+C creates.
- Alt+D requests eligible deletion.
- Esc returns to Dashboard.

Alteration
- Loads header, JWO/component links, quantities, rates, statutory details and narration.
- Voucher number is immutable after save.
- Existing voucher quantity is excluded from pending validation during alteration.
- Reposts stock movements atomically.
- Blocks alteration when an active downstream voucher such as Material In is linked.
- Uses concurrency-token protection.

Deletion
- Native TexTrack confirmation modal.
- Allowed only for an active, unlinked Material Out voucher.
- Deletes linked stock movements and JWO link in one transaction.
- Reopens JWO pending quantity and updates JWO status.
- Cancelled vouchers remain protected from deletion.

Cancellation
- Open voucher and press Alt+X.
- Native modal with mandatory reason.
- Preserves voucher and voucher number.
- Adds reversal stock movements and reopens JWO pending quantity.
- Records reason, user, date/time and audit entry.
- Blocks cancellation when an active downstream Material In/dependent voucher exists.

Universal shortcut foundation
- App.razor now recognises data-voucher-cancel-action="true" for Alt+X.
- Material Out uses the shared shortcut contract. Future voucher pages should expose the same attribute.

Migration
- 010_material_out_alter_cancel.sql expands allowed stock movement kinds for cancellation reversals.

Compilation note
- .NET SDK was unavailable in the packaging environment. No compilation claim is made.
