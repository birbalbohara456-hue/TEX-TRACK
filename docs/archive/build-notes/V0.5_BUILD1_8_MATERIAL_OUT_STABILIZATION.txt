TexTrack ERP v0.5 Build 1.8 — Material Out Stabilization

Fixes:
1. Material Out Esc now opens a local native TexTrack exit modal and does not navigate until Yes is explicitly chosen.
2. No/Esc closes the modal and keeps the voucher open.
3. Material Out page key events no longer bubble into the layout and trigger a second Escape action.
4. Rate is prefilled from the selected JWO component rate, displayed as an editable field, and retained as a Material Out voucher snapshot.
5. Quantity Enter moves to Rate; Rate Enter moves to the next component quantity or GST/e-Way Bill selector.
6. Amount recalculates from Issue Quantity × Rate.

No database migration or repository posting architecture was changed.
