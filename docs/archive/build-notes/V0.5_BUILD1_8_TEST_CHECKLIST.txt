TexTrack ERP v0.5 Build 1.8 — Focused Test Checklist

A. Exit modal
1. Open Material Out and load a JWO.
2. Press Esc once.
3. Confirm the voucher remains open and the native Exit Voucher modal appears.
4. Press Enter while No is selected: modal closes, voucher stays open.
5. Open modal again, choose Yes using Left/Right or Tab, press Enter: return to Dashboard.
6. Confirm no modal remains visible after navigation and keyboard focus is active on Dashboard.

B. Rate
1. Create a JWO component with a non-zero rate and save it.
2. Load it in Material Out.
3. Confirm Rate is prefilled from JWO.
4. Edit Rate using keyboard.
5. Confirm Amount immediately follows Quantity × Rate.
6. Press Enter from Quantity: focus moves to Rate.
7. Press Enter from Rate: focus moves to next component Quantity or GST/e-Way Bill.
8. Save and verify the Material Out line stores the edited rate without changing the JWO rate.
